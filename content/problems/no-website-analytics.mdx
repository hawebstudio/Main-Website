# No Website Analytics & Tracking

A website should help you make informed business decisions. Without analytics, it's difficult to understand how visitors interact with your website, which marketing efforts generate results, or where opportunities for improvement exist.

Analytics provides visibility into user behavior, while conversion tracking measures whether the website is achieving its business objectives.

See the connected [service-led solutions](/services) for the implementation approach behind this challenge.

## Signs You Need Better Analytics

Your website may lack proper tracking if:

- You don't know how many enquiries come from the website.
- Marketing performance cannot be measured.
- You cannot identify your best-performing pages.
- Customer journeys are unclear.
- Website changes are made without supporting data.
- There is no conversion tracking.

These limitations make it difficult to improve website performance over time.

## Common Causes

Analytics problems often occur because:

- Google Analytics wasn't configured correctly.
- Important events aren't tracked.
- Conversion goals are missing.
- Heatmaps and session recordings aren't available.
- Search Console isn't connected.
- Reports focus on traffic instead of business outcomes.

A complete analytics strategy goes beyond installing a tracking code.

## What Should Be Tracked?

A business website should typically measure:

- Contact form submissions
- Phone number clicks
- Email clicks
- Quote requests
- Purchases
- Appointment bookings
- Landing page performance
- Traffic sources
- User engagement

The exact events depend on your business goals.

## Our Approach

We begin by identifying the actions that matter most to your business.

This includes configuring:

- Google Analytics 4
- Microsoft Clarity
- Google Search Console
- Conversion events
- Custom event tracking
- Traffic source reporting
- Goal measurement
- Performance dashboards

The focus is on generating useful business insights rather than collecting unnecessary data.

## Decision Framework

Not every website requires complex reporting.

Some businesses only need basic conversion tracking, while others benefit from detailed event tracking, customer journey analysis, and ongoing reporting.

The analytics setup should match the complexity of your business and marketing activities.
