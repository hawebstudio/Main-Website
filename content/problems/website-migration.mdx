# Need to Migrate My Website

Migrating a website involves much more than moving files from one server to another. It affects search visibility, website performance, content management, integrations, and business continuity.

A successful migration should improve the website without disrupting customers or losing valuable search engine visibility.

See the connected [service-led solutions](/services) to explore the implementation approach behind this challenge.

## Signs You Need a Website Migration

A migration may be appropriate if:

- Your current CMS no longer meets your needs.
- Hosting has become unreliable.
- The website is difficult to maintain.
- You're rebranding your business.
- Performance has become a recurring issue.
- You want to modernize your technology stack.

Migration is often part of a larger business or technical transition.

## Common Challenges

Website migrations can introduce issues if they are not planned carefully.

Common risks include:

- Broken links
- Lost search rankings
- Missing content
- Downtime
- Broken forms
- Incorrect redirects
- Analytics tracking failures
- Integration problems

A structured migration process helps reduce these risks.

## What Should Be Preserved?

During migration, important assets should be maintained wherever possible.

This includes:

- Existing URLs
- SEO metadata
- Images and media
- Internal links
- Redirects
- Analytics
- Search Console configuration
- Structured data

Preserving these elements helps maintain continuity after launch.

## Our Approach

Every migration starts with a technical audit.

We review:

- Existing architecture
- Content inventory
- CMS
- Hosting
- DNS
- SSL
- Technical SEO
- Third-party integrations

Only after understanding the current website do we plan the migration strategy.

## Decision Framework

Not every website requires migration.

Sometimes targeted improvements, infrastructure upgrades, or CMS modernization provide better value than changing the entire platform.

The best solution depends on your current website, business goals, and future plans.

If your current website is limiting your business or you're planning to move to a modern platform, a structured migration can improve performance, maintainability, and long-term scalability while protecting your existing online presence.
