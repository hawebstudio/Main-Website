# Need Website Maintenance & Support

Launching a website is the beginning of its lifecycle, not the end. As browsers, search engines, frameworks, and customer expectations evolve, websites require ongoing maintenance to remain secure, reliable, and effective.

Without regular maintenance, even a well-built website can gradually become slower, less secure, and more difficult to manage.

See the connected [service-led solutions](/services) for the implementation approach behind this challenge.

## Signs Your Website Needs Maintenance

Your website may require ongoing support if:

- Software updates are regularly postponed.
- Security patches have not been applied.
- Performance has declined over time.
- Forms or integrations occasionally stop working.
- Backups are inconsistent or unavailable.
- Nobody actively monitors the website.

These issues often become more expensive to resolve when left unattended.

## Common Maintenance Tasks

A healthy website requires routine attention.

Typical maintenance includes:

- Software updates
- Security monitoring
- Backup management
- Performance optimization
- Broken link checks
- Analytics reviews
- Technical SEO audits
- Uptime monitoring
- SSL certificate monitoring
- Infrastructure maintenance

These activities help reduce the risk of unexpected issues.

## Why Maintenance Matters

Regular maintenance helps:

- Improve website security
- Maintain fast loading speeds
- Reduce downtime
- Keep integrations functioning correctly
- Preserve search visibility
- Extend the lifespan of the website

Proactive maintenance is generally more cost-effective than emergency repairs.

## Our Approach

We take a preventative approach to website maintenance.

This includes reviewing:

- Security
- Performance
- Backups
- Analytics
- Technical SEO
- Infrastructure
- Third-party integrations
- Software updates

Rather than reacting only after something breaks, regular reviews help identify potential issues early.

## Decision Framework

Not every website requires the same level of maintenance.

A small brochure website has different needs than an ecommerce store or custom web application.

The maintenance plan should reflect the website's complexity, traffic, and business importance.

## Next Steps

If your website hasn't been reviewed for several months — or you're unsure who is responsible for keeping it secure and up to date — it's worth establishing a regular maintenance process.
