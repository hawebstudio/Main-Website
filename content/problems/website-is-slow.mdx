# Why Your Website Is Slow (And How It's Costing You)

In the modern digital landscape, speed is not a luxury; it is a fundamental requirement. Users expect websites to load almost instantly. When they don't, frustration sets in, and users leave. 

A slow website is a silent killer for your business. It damages your brand perception, lowers your search engine rankings, and directly destroys conversion rates.

## The Business Cost of a Slow Website

The impact of poor performance is measurable and severe:

- **Lost Conversions**: Amazon's own internal testing found that every 100 milliseconds of added latency cost them roughly 1% in sales — a finding documented by former Amazon engineer Greg Linden. Small delays compound into real revenue loss at scale, and the same pattern holds for smaller businesses.
- **Plummeting SEO Rankings**: Google officially uses Core Web Vitals (speed, interactivity, and visual stability) as a ranking factor. If your site is slow, Google will penalize it in search results.
- **High Bounce Rates**: If a page takes longer than 3 seconds to load, over 50% of mobile users will abandon the site. You are paying for traffic (through ads or SEO) only to lose it at the front door.
- **Damaged Brand Trust**: A sluggish, clunky website feels unprofessional. Users associate the quality of your digital experience with the quality of your services.

## Common Root Causes of Slow Websites

Website performance degrades for several predictable reasons:

**Oversized, Unoptimised Images**: Uploading massive, multi-megabyte images directly from a camera or stock site is the #1 cause of slow load times.

**Cheap, Shared Hosting**: Budget hosting crams thousands of websites onto a single server. When one gets busy, yours slows down.

**Plugin and Script Bloat**: Every plugin, marketing tracker, chat widget, and analytics script you add requires the browser to download and execute more code.

**Render-Blocking Resources**: Heavy CSS and JavaScript files that must load before the page can even begin to display content.

**Lack of Caching and CDNs**: Failing to serve content from servers geographically close to the user, or forcing the server to rebuild the page from scratch on every single visit.

## How We Optimise Performance

Fixing a slow website requires a systematic, technical approach. We don't just install a caching plugin and hope for the best.

**Comprehensive Auditing**: We analyse your site using Lighthouse, WebPageTest, and Chrome User Experience Report (CrUX) data to pinpoint exactly what is causing the delays.

**Asset Optimisation**: We compress and convert images to modern formats (like WebP or AVIF), implement lazy loading so media only loads when scrolled into view, and minify CSS/JS files.

**Infrastructure Upgrades**: Where necessary, we migrate sites to premium, edge-network hosting environments (like Vercel) and configure Content Delivery Networks (CDNs).

**Code Refactoring**: We defer non-critical scripts, eliminate render-blocking resources, and strip out unnecessary bloated code.

**Core Web Vitals Focus**: We specifically target Google's key metrics: ensuring the Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) happens quickly, Interaction to Next Paint (INP) is responsive, and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) is eliminated.
