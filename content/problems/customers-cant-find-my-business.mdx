# Customers Can't Find My Business

For many businesses, local customers are the most valuable source of enquiries. If your business isn't appearing when people search for services in your area, you're likely missing opportunities to connect with potential customers.

Local visibility depends on more than simply having a website. Search engines evaluate your website, business profile, location information, reviews, and technical signals to determine which businesses appear in local search results.

See the connected [service-led solutions](/services) to explore the implementation options behind this challenge.

## Signs You Have a Local Visibility Problem

You may have a local search problem if:

- Your business rarely appears on Google Maps.
- Competitors consistently rank above you.
- Google Business Profile receives few interactions.
- Customers say they couldn't find your business online.
- Website traffic from local searches is low.
- You receive very few calls or direction requests.

These symptoms often indicate issues with local SEO rather than your business itself.

## Common Causes

Poor local visibility can result from:

- Incomplete Google Business Profile
- Inconsistent business information
- Missing Local Business schema
- Weak location-based content
- Few customer reviews
- Technical SEO issues
- Poor website performance
- Limited local authority

Addressing these issues together creates a stronger local search presence.

## What Should Be Improved?

Improving local visibility often includes:

- Google Business Profile optimization
- Accurate business information
- Local landing pages
- Local Business schema
- Technical SEO
- Review management
- Website performance
- Citation consistency

These improvements help search engines better understand your business and its service area.

## Our Approach

This includes reviewing:

- Google Business Profile
- Website structure

The objective is to identify the factors limiting your local discoverability before recommending improvements.

## Decision Framework

Not every local visibility issue requires a website redesign.

Some businesses benefit most from improving their Google Business Profile, while others need technical SEO, stronger location pages, or better website performance.

A structured review helps determine the most effective approach.
