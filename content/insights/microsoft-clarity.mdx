# Microsoft Clarity: Understanding How Visitors Actually Use Your Website

Traditional analytics tells you **what** users do. Microsoft Clarity helps you understand **how** they do it. By combining session recordings, heatmaps, and interaction data, Clarity provides a visual understanding of how visitors navigate a website, where they hit friction, and which elements actually attract attention — instead of relying on assumptions.

See the connected [analytics & tracking setup](/services/analytics-tracking-setup) for the implementation path behind this topic.

## What Is Microsoft Clarity?

Microsoft Clarity is a free behavior analytics platform that records anonymous user interactions and visualizes engagement across a website. It helps answer questions GA4 alone doesn't: which sections get the most attention, where visitors click, how far they scroll, which pages cause confusion, and where they abandon the site.

## Session Recordings

Session recordings replay anonymous visits, surfacing navigation patterns, hesitation before an action, rapid page exits, repeated clicks, form abandonment, and other unexpected behavior — context that's hard to get from numbers alone.

## Heatmaps

**Click heatmaps** show where visitors click most, useful for evaluating navigation, buttons, CTAs, and images. **Scroll heatmaps** show how far visitors actually get down a page, revealing whether important content or CTAs are seen at all. **Area heatmaps** highlight which sections get the most attention, informing future layout decisions.

## Identifying Friction

Clarity automatically flags behaviors that usually indicate a usability problem:

- **Dead clicks** — users click something expecting an action, and nothing happens.
- **Rage clicks** — repeated clicks on the same spot out of frustration or confusion.
- **Excessive scrolling** — searching for information that isn't clearly presented.
- **Quick back navigation** — leaving immediately after arriving, suggesting the page didn't meet expectations.

## Practical Takeaway

|          | Google Analytics 4                             | Microsoft Clarity                                       |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Measures | Traffic sources, sessions, conversions, events | Session recordings, heatmaps, click and scroll patterns |
| Answers  | What happened                                  | Why it happened                                         |

Used together, they cover both sides of the same question. Behavior insights typically inform simplifying navigation, moving important information higher, improving CTA placement, redesigning forms, and optimizing mobile layouts — small changes that often have an outsized impact on conversion.

## Implementation Notes

A responsible implementation includes consent management where required, anonymous session recording, no collection of sensitive personal information, and clear privacy policy disclosure. Best practice is reviewing recordings regularly, focusing on high-traffic and conversion-critical pages, comparing desktop and mobile behavior, and specifically investigating rage clicks and dead clicks — then re-evaluating after any major site update. See [Google Analytics 4](/insights/google-analytics-4) for how this pairs with quantitative tracking, and [Conversion Rate Optimization](/insights/conversion-rate-optimization-guide) for how these findings turn into actual site changes.
