# Google Search Console
## What Is Google Search Console?

Google Search Console is a free tool from Google that shows exactly how your website appears and performs in Google Search: which queries bring visitors to your site, your click-through rate, your average ranking position, and any technical issues Google encountered while crawling your pages.

At HA Web Studio, Search Console is set up on every website we launch, giving direct visibility into how Google actually sees and ranks your site.

## Why We Use Google Search Console at HA Web Studio

- Direct visibility into search queries driving traffic to your site
- Early detection of indexing errors and crawl issues
- Sitemap submission to help Google discover your pages faster
- Mobile usability and Core Web Vitals reporting straight from Google
- Manual action alerts if Google flags a policy issue with your site

## How Google Search Console Helps Your Business

SEO work without visibility into Search Console is guesswork. It tells you exactly which search terms are already bringing visitors, so content and SEO efforts can focus on what's already showing traction rather than starting blind.

It also catches problems before they cost you traffic: a page accidentally blocked from indexing, a spike in crawl errors, or a mobile usability issue, all of which can quietly hurt your rankings if left unnoticed.

## Google Search Console Use Cases We Build

- Sitemap submission and indexing monitoring for new and existing sites
- Search query and ranking analysis to guide content strategy
- Technical issue detection (crawl errors, mobile usability, security issues)
- Core Web Vitals monitoring at the page level
- Structured data and rich result monitoring

## Google Search Console + Our Stack

Search Console pairs with Google Analytics 4 for a complete picture of both how visitors find you and what they do once they arrive, and with Schema.org structured data, which Search Console reports on directly for rich result eligibility.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do we need Search Console if we already have Google Analytics?**
Yes, they show different things. Analytics shows on-site behavior; Search Console shows how you appear and perform in search results before a visitor even clicks.

**Will this tell us if something is wrong with our SEO?**
Yes, it's often the first place a technical SEO issue, like accidental de-indexing, shows up.

**Is Search Console free?**
Yes, it's a free tool from Google, we handle the setup and ongoing monitoring as part of our SEO work.
