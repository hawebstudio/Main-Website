# Schema.org

## Overview

Schema.org is a shared vocabulary for structured data, a standardized way of labeling content a product, a review, a business address, a FAQ so search engines can understand it precisely, rather than inferring meaning from plain text alone. Implemented correctly, it's what enables rich results in Google: star ratings, pricing, FAQ dropdowns, and business information shown directly in search listings.

## What Is Schema.org?

Schema.org is a shared vocabulary for structured data, a standardized way of labeling content (a product, a review, a business address, a FAQ) so search engines can understand it precisely, rather than inferring meaning from plain text alone. Implemented correctly, it's what enables rich results in Google: star ratings, pricing, FAQ dropdowns, and business information shown directly in search listings.

At HA Web Studio, we implement Schema.org markup on every website, tailored to the specific content types your business has.

## Why We Use Schema.org at HA Web Studio

- Enables rich results (star ratings, FAQs, pricing) directly in search listings
- Helps AI-powered search tools understand and cite your content accurately
- Clarifies business information (address, hours, contact) for local search
- Improves how confidently search engines can categorize your content
- Increasingly important as AI answer engines rely on structured data

## How Schema.org Helps Your Business

Rich results, star ratings under a search listing, an FAQ dropdown, a visible price, make your listing stand out and typically improve click-through rate compared to a plain blue link, even at the same ranking position.

As AI-powered search and answer engines become a bigger source of traffic, structured data is also becoming one of the clearest signals these systems use to understand and accurately represent your business, which matters for showing up correctly in AI-generated answers, not just traditional search results.

## Schema.org Use Cases We Build

- LocalBusiness markup for local SEO (address, hours, service area)
- Product and Review markup for e-commerce rich results
- FAQ and HowTo markup for informational content
- Article and BlogPosting markup for content sites
- Organization markup for brand and company information

## Schema.org + Our Stack

We implement Schema.org as JSON-LD directly in the page markup of every Next.js and WordPress site we build, verified through Google Search Console's rich results reporting to confirm it's read correctly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will structured data guarantee rich results in Google?**
No, it makes you eligible; Google still decides when and whether to display rich results, but proper markup significantly improves your chances.

**Is this only useful for e-commerce?**
No, structured data applies to nearly any content type, business information, articles, FAQs, and events all benefit.

**Does this affect how AI tools like ChatGPT or Google's AI Overviews represent our business?**
Yes, structured data is one of the clearer signals these systems use to accurately understand and cite your content.
