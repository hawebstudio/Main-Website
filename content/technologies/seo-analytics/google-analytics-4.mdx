# Google Analytics 4
## What Is Google Analytics 4?

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is Google's current analytics platform, tracking how visitors find and interact with your website: which pages they visit, how they arrived, and what actions (form submissions, purchases, sign-ups) they complete. It replaced Universal Analytics with an event-based model built around user behavior rather than simple pageviews.

At HA Web Studio, GA4 is set up on every website we build, configured correctly from launch so the data it collects is actually reliable and useful for decisions.

## Why We Use Google Analytics 4 at HA Web Studio

- Event-based tracking that captures meaningful actions, not just pageviews
- Free, industry-standard analytics with deep integration into Google's ecosystem
- Custom conversion tracking for forms, purchases, and sign-ups
- Audience and traffic source data to understand where visitors come from
- Foundation for measuring the return on your marketing spend

## How Google Analytics 4 Helps Your Business

Without accurate analytics, marketing decisions are guesses. GA4, set up correctly, tells you exactly which pages convert, which traffic sources bring in real customers, and where visitors drop off before completing a form or purchase, information that directly informs where to invest marketing budget.

Many businesses have GA4 installed but misconfigured, missing key conversion events or double-counting traffic. We configure it specifically around your business goals, so the data reflects what's actually happening.

## Google Analytics 4 Use Cases We Build

- Conversion tracking for forms, calls, and purchases
- E-commerce tracking for product views, cart activity, and sales
- Traffic source and campaign attribution

## Google Analytics 4 + Our Stack

We implement GA4 alongside Google Search Console and Microsoft Clarity for a full picture: GA4 shows what happened, Clarity shows how visitors actually behaved, and Search Console shows how you're found. Together, this gives a complete, trustworthy view of your site's performance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is GA4 different from the old Google Analytics?**
Yes, significantly. GA4 uses an event-based model and different reporting, which is why proper reconfiguration, not just a copy-paste setup, matters.

**Will you set up conversion tracking specific to our business?**
Yes, we configure GA4 around your actual goals, whether that's form submissions, phone calls, or online purchases.

**Do we need Google Analytics if we already have another tool?**
GA4 is free and integrates deeply with Google Ads and Search Console, making it a strong baseline even alongside other analytics tools.
