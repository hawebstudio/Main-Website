# Lighthouse
## What Is Lighthouse?

Lighthouse is Google's open-source auditing tool that scores a website across performance, accessibility, best practices, and SEO, the same underlying signals Google uses when evaluating Core Web Vitals and page experience for ranking purposes.

At HA Web Studio, Lighthouse audits are part of our standard process for every website, both during development and as an ongoing performance check after launch.

## Why We Use Lighthouse at HA Web Studio

- Direct measurement of the Core Web Vitals that affect Google rankings
- Accessibility scoring to catch issues before launch
- SEO best practice checks (meta tags, crawlability, mobile-friendliness)
- Clear, actionable recommendations for specific improvements
- Same tool Google itself uses to evaluate page experience

## How Lighthouse Helps Your Business

Page speed and technical quality directly affect both your Google rankings and how many visitors stick around long enough to convert. Lighthouse gives us a concrete, measurable score rather than a vague sense of whether a site "feels fast," which lets us target specific issues, an oversized image, unused JavaScript, a layout shift, and verify the fix actually worked.

Running this audit before launch, rather than discovering performance problems after the fact through lost rankings or high bounce rates, saves real time and avoids costly post-launch fixes.

## Lighthouse Use Cases We Build

- Pre-launch performance and accessibility audits on every project
- Core Web Vitals optimization (loading speed, interactivity, visual stability)
- Ongoing performance monitoring after significant content or design updates
- Accessibility issue detection before compliance becomes a legal risk
- Mobile performance verification, where most traffic now occurs

## Lighthouse + Our Stack

Lighthouse audits guide the performance decisions we make across our stack: image optimization in Next.js, lean CSS through Tailwind, and efficient loading strategies, all measured and verified against the same benchmarks Google uses.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What's a good Lighthouse score?**
Generally, scores above 90 across categories are considered strong; we target this range on every project we build.

**Does a bad Lighthouse score directly hurt our Google ranking?**
Core Web Vitals, which Lighthouse measures, are a confirmed ranking factor, though not the only one. Poor scores also directly hurt conversion by creating a slow, frustrating experience.

**Will you fix issues Lighthouse finds, or just report them?**
We fix them. Lighthouse audits are part of our build and QA process, not just a report we hand over.
