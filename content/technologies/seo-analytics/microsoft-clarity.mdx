# Microsoft Clarity

## Overview

Microsoft Clarity is a free behavioral analytics tool that records anonymized user sessions and generates heatmaps showing exactly where visitors click, scroll, and pause on your website. Where Google Analytics tells you what happened a visitor left the checkout page, Clarity shows you how, watching the actual session replay.

## What Is Microsoft Clarity?

Microsoft Clarity is a free behavioral analytics tool that records anonymized user sessions and generates heatmaps showing exactly where visitors click, scroll, and pause on your website. Where Google Analytics tells you what happened (a visitor left the checkout page), Clarity shows you how, watching the actual session replay.

At HA Web Studio, we set up Clarity alongside Google Analytics 4 to give a fuller, more visual picture of how visitors actually experience your website.

## Why We Use Microsoft Clarity at HA Web Studio

- Free session recordings showing real visitor behavior
- Heatmaps revealing where attention and clicks concentrate on a page
- Rage-click and dead-click detection to flag frustrating interface issues
- No sampling limits, unlike some paid heatmap tools
- Direct visibility into why a page might not be converting

## How Microsoft Clarity Helps Your Business

Numbers alone don't always explain why visitors abandon a form or leave a page. Watching actual session recordings often reveals the real issue, a confusing button, a form field that's easy to miss, an element that looks clickable but isn't, in a way that a spreadsheet of numbers can't.

This visibility lets us make specific, evidence-based design changes rather than guessing at what might improve conversion, which is a more efficient way to spend design and development time.

## Microsoft Clarity Use Cases We Build

- Diagnosing why a specific page or form has a high drop-off rate
- Identifying confusing interface elements through rage-click detection
- Validating design changes with before-and-after session comparisons
- Understanding scroll depth on long-form content pages
- Spotting mobile-specific usability issues

## Microsoft Clarity + Our Stack

Clarity complements Google Analytics 4's quantitative data with qualitative, visual insight, together giving both the what and the why behind visitor behavior, which directly informs the design and conversion optimization work we do on your site.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Microsoft Clarity really free?**
Yes, it's a free tool with no meaningful usage limits, unlike many paid heatmap and session recording tools.

**Does this violate visitor privacy?**
Clarity anonymizes and masks sensitive data by default, and we configure it in line with standard privacy practices.

**Will this slow down our website?**
No, Clarity's tracking script is lightweight and designed not to meaningfully affect page load performance.
