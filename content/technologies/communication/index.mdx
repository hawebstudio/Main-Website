# Communication

## Introduction

This category covers sending email programmatically from a website or application — order
confirmations, password resets, contact-form notifications, and similar transactional
messages. It's a different job from marketing email tools: the focus here is reliable,
fast delivery of individual, triggered messages rather than bulk campaign sending. Resend and
SendGrid are both transactional email APIs built for developers to integrate directly into an
application; Brevo covers a similar space with added marketing-automation features when a
project needs both transactional and campaign email from one provider. Nodemailer is a
Node.js library for sending email through any SMTP provider, used when a project needs direct
control over the sending process rather than going through a dedicated transactional-email API.

## When These Technologies Are Used

Any site with a contact form, user account system, or checkout flow needs transactional email
— to confirm a submission, verify an account, or send a receipt. The choice between these
tools mostly comes down to deliverability requirements and existing infrastructure: Resend and
SendGrid are strong defaults for straightforward transactional needs, Brevo becomes relevant
when a client also wants newsletter or campaign functionality without adding a second tool, and
Nodemailer is used when a project already has its own SMTP relay or needs email-sending logic
that doesn't depend on a third-party API's specific feature set.
