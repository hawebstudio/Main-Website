# Resend
## What Is Resend?

Resend is a modern email delivery service built for developers, designed to reliably send transactional emails, order confirmations, password resets, contact form notifications, from your website or application, with strong deliverability and clear tracking of whether emails actually arrived.

At HA Web Studio, Resend is our default choice for handling the automated emails a website needs to send, ensuring they reach your customers' inboxes rather than getting lost or filtered as spam.

## Why We Use Resend at HA Web Studio

- Strong deliverability, so emails actually reach the inbox, not spam folders
- Clean, well-documented API for straightforward integration
- Built-in tracking of email delivery, opens, and failures
- Support for well-designed, branded email templates
- Built specifically for developers building modern web applications

## How Resend Helps Your Business

An order confirmation or contact form notification that never arrives is a real business problem, a customer left uncertain their order went through, or a lead you never see because the notification landed in spam. Resend's focus on deliverability directly reduces this risk, and its tracking tells us clearly if an email failed to deliver.

Well-designed, branded transactional emails also reinforce trust and professionalism at exactly the moments your customers are paying closest attention: right after they've made a purchase or submitted an inquiry.

## Resend Use Cases We Build

- Order and booking confirmation emails
- Contact and quote form notifications to your team
- Password reset and account verification emails
- Automated follow-up sequences after a purchase or inquiry
- Branded email templates matching your website's design

## Resend + Our Stack

Resend integrates directly with our Node.js and Express backends, triggered by events like a form submission or completed order, with email templates built in React to match your website's branding exactly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will our emails end up in spam folders?**
Resend is built specifically to maximize deliverability, and we configure proper authentication (SPF, DKIM) to further reduce this risk.

**Can emails match our brand design?**
Yes, we build custom-branded email templates rather than using generic, unstyled default emails.

**How do we know if an email failed to send?**
Resend provides delivery tracking, and we can set up alerts for failed deliveries so nothing slips through unnoticed.
