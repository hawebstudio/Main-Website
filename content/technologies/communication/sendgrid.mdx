# SendGrid

## Overview

SendGrid is an email delivery platform for projects that need reliable, high-volume transactional email infrastructure. It is most appropriate when sending volume, delivery monitoring, and operational reliability matter more than keeping the setup minimal.

## What Is SendGrid?

SendGrid is an established email delivery platform, owned by Twilio, built to handle transactional and marketing email at high volume with enterprise-grade infrastructure, detailed analytics, and a long track record of reliability across large-scale sending.

At HA Web Studio, we use SendGrid for projects that need proven, high-volume email infrastructure, particularly businesses sending large amounts of transactional email or requiring the compliance and support options that come with an established enterprise platform.

## When SendGrid Fits a Project

SendGrid tends to be the right call once email volume, deliverability monitoring, or enterprise
compliance requirements become a real concern — not for a typical small-business contact form
or a handful of transactional emails per day. For most standard projects, a lighter
developer-first API like Resend is faster to integrate and easier to maintain, since SendGrid's
extra configuration and account-verification requirements exist to support scale most small
sites don't need. SendGrid is worth the added setup specifically when a project is sending at
meaningful volume, needs detailed delivery analytics, or benefits from Twilio's broader
communications ecosystem alongside email.

Its account setup process is also stricter than a lighter transactional API — sender
verification, domain authentication, and compliance checks are more involved, which is
reasonable at scale but adds friction for a project that just needs a handful of reliable
transactional emails going out. That trade-off is why it's positioned here as the choice for
higher-volume or compliance-sensitive projects specifically, rather than a default pick for
every integration.
