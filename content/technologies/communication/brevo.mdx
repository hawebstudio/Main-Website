# Brevo

## Overview

Brevo is an email platform for businesses that need transactional email, marketing campaigns, and automation in one place. It is useful when a website needs to send operational messages while also supporting newsletters, promotions, and segmented follow-up campaigns.

## What Is Brevo?

Brevo, formerly Sendinblue, combines transactional email, marketing campaigns, and automation in a single tool. A business can send order confirmations and password resets alongside newsletters and promotional campaigns without juggling separate services.

At HA Web Studio, we use Brevo when a business needs both reliable transactional email and ongoing marketing communication, without paying for and managing two entirely separate platforms.

## Why We Use It

- Combines transactional email and marketing campaigns in one platform
- Built-in marketing automation, contact lists, and campaign tracking
- Generous free tier that fits smaller businesses just getting started
- Clear reporting on opens, clicks, and campaign performance
- Straightforward API for triggering transactional email from your website

## How Brevo Helps Your Business

Many businesses need more than just order confirmations. They also want to send newsletters, promotions, and follow-up campaigns to build ongoing relationships with customers. Brevo covers both needs from one dashboard, so your team can manage transactional and marketing email without switching tools or reconciling data across platforms.

This consolidation can also reduce cost for growing businesses, since a single Brevo subscription can replace what would otherwise be two separate services.

## Typical Use Cases

- Order and booking confirmation emails
- Newsletter signups and ongoing email marketing campaigns
- Automated follow-up sequences after a purchase or inquiry
- Contact list segmentation for targeted promotions
- Combined transactional and marketing email under one account

## Key Features

- Transactional email API
- Email marketing campaigns
- Contact list management
- Marketing automation workflows
- Campaign performance reporting

## Where We Use It

Brevo integrates with Node.js and Express backends for transactional email triggered by site events. Its marketing tools can connect to the same contact data your website collects, so signups and customer information flow into campaigns without manual exporting.

## FAQ

### Do we need Brevo if we already have Resend?

Not usually. Brevo is worth considering when you also want marketing campaigns and newsletters alongside transactional email, rather than transactional email alone.

### Can we manage both order emails and newsletters in one place?

Yes. That is Brevo's core strength: combining transactional and marketing email into a single platform and contact database.

### Is Brevo suitable for a small business just starting out?

Yes. Its free tier and straightforward setup make it a practical starting point for smaller businesses.
