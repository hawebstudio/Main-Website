# Sanity
## What Is Sanity?

Sanity is a headless content management system: it stores and manages your content (pages, blog posts, product descriptions, images) separately from how that content is displayed. Your team edits content through a customizable studio interface, while the actual website, built with Next.js in our case, pulls that content in and renders it however it's designed to look.

At HA Web Studio, Sanity is our preferred CMS when a client needs a genuinely custom editing experience and fast, flexible content delivery.

## Why We Use Sanity at HA Web Studio

- Fully customizable editing interface tailored to your content types
- Structured content that can be reused across web, mobile, or other channels
- Fast content delivery through a globally distributed API
- Real-time collaboration for teams editing content together
- Decoupled from the frontend, so your site's design isn't limited by the CMS

## How Sanity Helps Your Business

A CMS should make it easy for your team to update content without needing a developer for every change, a blog post, a new service page, updated pricing. Sanity's studio can be customized to match exactly how your business thinks about its content, which reduces training time and editing mistakes.

Because Sanity separates content from presentation, your website's design and performance aren't constrained by the CMS the way they often are with more rigid, all-in-one platforms.

## Sanity Use Cases We Build

- Custom blogs and content hubs with editor-friendly interfaces
- Marketing sites with frequently updated landing pages
- Multi-language and multi-region content structures
- Product catalogs with rich, structured content
- Content that needs to power both a website and other channels

## Sanity + Our Stack

Sanity pairs directly with Next.js, which fetches content through Sanity's API and renders it with React, styled with Tailwind CSS. This combination gives your team an easy editing experience and your visitors a fast, SEO-friendly website.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will our team need training to use Sanity?**
The studio is built around your specific content types, so it's generally intuitive, and we provide a walkthrough as part of every project.

**Can Sanity handle a large content library?**
Yes, it's built to scale to large volumes of structured content across multiple content types and languages.

**Is Sanity better than WordPress?**
For teams that want a highly custom editing experience paired with a fast, code-first frontend, yes. WordPress remains a strong choice for simpler, all-in-one content management.
