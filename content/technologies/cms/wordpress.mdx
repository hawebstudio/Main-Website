# WordPress
## What Is WordPress?

WordPress is the world's most widely used content management system, powering a large share of websites on the internet. It provides an all-in-one platform for managing pages, blog posts, media, and, through WooCommerce, e-commerce, with a familiar admin dashboard that millions of people already know how to use.

At HA Web Studio, we build custom WordPress websites for businesses that want a reliable, easy-to-manage platform and a team that's already comfortable with it.

## Why We Use WordPress at HA Web Studio

- Familiar, easy-to-learn admin dashboard for content editors
- Massive plugin ecosystem covering nearly any functionality need
- Well suited to content-heavy sites and blogs
- Strong SEO plugin support for on-page optimization
- Large talent pool for future support and maintenance

## How WordPress Helps Your Business

For businesses without an in-house development team, WordPress means your staff can add blog posts, update pages, and manage media directly, without needing a developer for routine content changes. This keeps your website active and current without ongoing agency costs for every small update.

We build custom themes rather than relying on generic page builders, which keeps your site fast, on-brand, and free of the bloat that often comes with heavily plugin-dependent WordPress sites.

## WordPress Use Cases We Build

- Business and corporate websites with frequent content updates
- Blogs and content marketing hubs
- WooCommerce-powered online stores
- Multi-author publishing sites
- Websites requiring extensive third-party plugin integrations

## WordPress + Our Stack

We build custom WordPress themes rather than using off-the-shelf builders, optimizing for performance and Core Web Vitals, and integrating SEO tooling, Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, and Schema.org markup, directly into the build.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is WordPress secure?**
Yes, when properly maintained, kept updated, and configured with security best practices, which we set up and can maintain on an ongoing basis.

**Will our WordPress site be slow?**
Not if built correctly. We avoid bloated page builders and unnecessary plugins, optimizing images and caching to keep load times fast.

**Can our team manage the site without calling us for every change?**
Yes, that's one of WordPress's core strengths, we design the admin experience so your team can confidently manage day-to-day content.
