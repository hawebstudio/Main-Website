# Payload CMS

## Overview

Payload is a code-first, self-hosted headless CMS built on Node.js and TypeScript. Unlike hosted CMS platforms, Payload runs on infrastructure you control, giving full ownership of your data, and its schema and access rules are defined directly in code, which keeps your content structure version-controlled alongside the rest of your project.

## What Is Payload CMS?

Payload is a code-first, self-hosted headless CMS built on Node.js and TypeScript. Unlike hosted CMS platforms, Payload runs on infrastructure you control, giving full ownership of your data, and its schema and access rules are defined directly in code, which keeps your content structure version-controlled alongside the rest of your project.

At HA Web Studio, we use Payload for clients who want a highly customizable CMS with full data ownership, without the ongoing costs of some hosted CMS platforms.

## Why We Use Payload CMS at HA Web Studio

- Full ownership of your data, self-hosted rather than tied to a vendor
- Content schema defined in TypeScript, version-controlled with your codebase
- Built-in authentication and role-based access control
- Auto-generated REST and GraphQL APIs from your content schema
- No per-seat or per-record pricing common with hosted CMS platforms

## How Payload CMS Helps Your Business

For businesses concerned about long-term costs or wanting full control over where their content lives, Payload removes dependency on a third-party hosted platform's pricing and policies. Your content, and the infrastructure it runs on, belongs entirely to you.

Because the schema lives in code, changes to your content structure go through the same review and deployment process as the rest of your website, reducing the risk of untracked, ad-hoc changes to how your content is organized.

## Payload CMS Use Cases We Build

- Content-heavy websites where long-term data ownership matters
- Applications needing custom roles and permissions for content editors
- Projects wanting to avoid recurring per-seat CMS licensing costs
- Multi-tenant or multi-brand content platforms
- Teams comfortable maintaining their own hosting infrastructure

## Payload CMS + Our Stack

Payload runs on Node.js and connects naturally with MongoDB or PostgreSQL, integrating with our Next.js and React frontend to deliver content through its auto-generated APIs, with TypeScript providing type safety from the CMS through to the rendered page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do we need our own server to run Payload?**
Yes, since it's self-hosted, we set up and manage the hosting (often on Render or similar infrastructure) as part of the project.

**Is Payload as easy to use as hosted CMS platforms?**
The editing interface is built to be intuitive for content editors; the added complexity is mainly on the technical/hosting side, which we handle.

**Why choose Payload over Sanity or WordPress?**
Payload makes sense when full data ownership, custom access control, and avoiding recurring platform fees are priorities for your business.
