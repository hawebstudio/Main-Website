# Cloudflare

## Overview

Cloudflare is a global network that sits between your website and its visitors, providing content delivery, DNS management, security protection like DDoS mitigation, and performance optimization. It's one of the largest networks of its kind, used to speed up and protect a significant share of websites on the internet.

## What Is Cloudflare?

Cloudflare is a global network that sits between your website and its visitors, providing content delivery, DNS management, security protection (like DDoS mitigation), and performance optimization. It's one of the largest networks of its kind, used to speed up and protect a significant share of websites on the internet.

At HA Web Studio, we use Cloudflare to manage DNS, add a layer of security, and improve load times for the websites we build, regardless of where they're ultimately hosted.

## Why We Use Cloudflare at HA Web Studio

- Global content delivery network that speeds up load times for visitors everywhere
- Protection against DDoS attacks and other common security threats
- Reliable, fast DNS management
- Free SSL certificates for secure, encrypted connections
- Additional caching and optimization layered on top of your hosting

## How Cloudflare Helps Your Business

Security incidents and downtime cost businesses real money and trust, a site knocked offline during a traffic spike or attack means lost leads and sales during exactly the moments visibility matters most. Cloudflare's network absorbs and mitigates many of these threats before they ever reach your actual server.

Performance improvements from Cloudflare's caching and global network also compound with your existing hosting, giving visitors faster load times regardless of their location, which supports both conversion rates and search rankings.

## Cloudflare Use Cases We Build

- Additional caching layer for improved global load times

## Cloudflare + Our Stack

Cloudflare typically sits in front of sites hosted on Vercel, Render, or self-hosted WordPress and WooCommerce installations, adding a security and performance layer regardless of the underlying hosting platform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Cloudflare a replacement for our hosting?**
No, it works alongside your hosting provider, adding security, DNS, and performance benefits rather than replacing where your site is actually hosted.

**Do we need Cloudflare if we're already on Vercel?**
It's often still worth adding for DNS management and an extra layer of security, particularly for domain configuration and protection against certain attack types.

**Is Cloudflare's free plan enough for most businesses?**
For many small to mid-sized businesses, yes; we advise on whether a paid tier makes sense based on your specific traffic and security needs.
