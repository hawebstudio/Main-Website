# HTML5

## Overview

HTML5 is the markup language that structures every page on the web. It defines the headings, paragraphs, forms, images, and sections that make up a website, and gives browsers and search engines a clear map of what's on the page.

## What Is HTML5?

HTML5 is the markup language that structures every page on the web. It defines the headings, paragraphs, forms, images, and sections that make up a website, and gives browsers and search engines a clear map of what's on the page.

At HA Web Studio, HTML5 is the foundation of every website we build, even when the site is powered by React or Next.js underneath. A well-structured HTML5 document is what allows a browser to render your site correctly on any device, and it's what allows Google to understand what your business actually does.

Modern HTML5 also includes native support for video, audio, form validation, and offline storage, which means fewer third-party plugins and faster-loading pages.

## Why We Use HTML5 at HA Web Studio

- Semantic tags (header, nav, main, article, section, footer) instead of generic divs
- Built-in accessibility hooks that work with screen readers
- Native form validation, reducing the need for extra JavaScript
- Clean markup that search engines can crawl and index without confusion
- No licensing cost and universal browser support

## How HTML5 Helps Your Business

A website with proper HTML5 structure loads faster, ranks better, and works for every visitor, including those using assistive technology. Search engines rely on semantic HTML to understand which text is a heading, which is a product description, and which is navigation. Get that structure right, and your content has a real chance of ranking.

Semantic HTML5 also makes a site easier to maintain. When your website needs a redesign or a new page a year from now, clean markup means our team can work with it quickly, which keeps future maintenance costs down.

## HTML5 Use Cases We Build

- Business and corporate websites with clear content hierarchy
- Landing pages structured for conversion and fast load times
- Accessible forms for bookings, quotes, and contact requests

## HTML5 + Our Stack

We rarely hand-code raw HTML5 for full projects today; instead, it's the output of frameworks like React and Next.js, which we use to build component-based websites. But the discipline of writing semantic, accessible HTML5 carries through everything we build, whether it's a simple landing page or a full Next.js application connected to a headless CMS.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does HTML5 affect my Google ranking?**
Indirectly, yes. Search engines use your HTML structure to understand your content. Poor structure makes it harder for Google to rank your pages correctly, even if the content itself is good.

**Is HTML5 outdated compared to frameworks like React?**
No. React and Next.js still generate HTML5 in the browser. Frameworks change how you build the page, not what the browser ultimately renders.

**Will my site work on old browsers?**
Yes. HTML5 has near-universal support, and we build with graceful fallbacks for any features that aren't supported everywhere.
