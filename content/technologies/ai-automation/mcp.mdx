# MCP (Model Context Protocol)
## What Is MCP?

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard for connecting AI models to external tools, data sources, and systems in a structured, secure way. Rather than every AI application needing a custom, one-off integration to connect to your CRM, calendar, or database, MCP provides a common protocol that both the AI model and the connected tool can speak.

At HA Web Studio, we use MCP when a project needs an AI assistant to reliably interact with real business systems, not just answer general questions.

## Why We Use MCP at HA Web Studio

- Standardized way to connect AI models to your actual business tools
- Reduces custom integration work for each new tool an AI assistant needs
- Clear boundaries around what data and actions an AI assistant can access
- Growing industry adoption across major AI providers
- Positions your systems to work with future AI tools without a rebuild

## How MCP Helps Your Business

An AI assistant that can only talk in general terms has limited practical value. MCP is what allows an assistant to actually check your calendar, look up an order status, or update a record in your CRM, safely and with clear permission boundaries, turning a conversational feature into something that gets real work done.

Because MCP is an emerging open standard rather than a single vendor's proprietary approach, building on it now means your integrations are more likely to remain compatible with new AI tools and models as the space develops.

## MCP Use Cases We Build

- AI assistants that check order status or account information
- Internal tools where AI needs access to company data with clear permissions
- Automation connecting AI models to scheduling, CRM, or inventory systems
- Assistants that can take defined actions (booking, updating records) safely
- Future-proofing AI integrations against a single vendor's proprietary approach

## MCP + Our Stack

MCP connects our AI integrations, built with Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini through the Vercel AI SDK or LangChain, to your actual business systems: databases, CRMs, and internal APIs, with clearly scoped access rather than open-ended permissions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this safe? Can the AI access anything it wants?**
No, MCP integrations are built with clearly defined boundaries around exactly what data and actions are accessible, which we design specifically around your business's needs.

**Do we need this if we just want a simple chatbot?**
Not necessarily. MCP becomes valuable when an AI assistant needs to interact with real systems, not just answer general questions.

**Is this a proprietary technology we'd be locked into?**
No, MCP is an open standard being adopted across the AI industry, which reduces long-term lock-in risk.
