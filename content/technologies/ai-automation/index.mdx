# AI & Automation

## Introduction

This category covers practical AI and workflow automation technologies implemented to improve data retrieval, streamline repetitive business processes, and enhance search engine visibility. Rather than adopting speculative tools for novelty, HA Web Studio evaluates AI frameworks strictly by their ability to solve defined operational friction: reducing manual data entry, accelerating knowledge access, and ensuring consistent structured output.

Our tooling includes developer frameworks like the Vercel AI SDK and LangChain for structured LLM interactions, frontier foundation models (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, and Google Gemini) accessed via secure APIs, and retrieval systems utilizing vector databases and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to ground AI outputs in authoritative internal documents.

## When These Technologies Are Used

AI and automation components are integrated into client projects only when simpler deterministic methods are insufficient. Common production use cases include:

- **Intelligent Knowledge Retrieval & RAG:** Connecting internal company handbooks, product catalogues, or support documentation to search systems so staff and users can find accurate answers quickly without hallucinations.
- **Workflow & Process Automation:** Automating multi-step operational chains—such as parsing inbound inquiry forms, enriching lead profiles, routing requests to relevant CRM pipelines, and dispatching transactional notifications via WhatsApp or email.
- **Structured Data & Content Enhancement:** Generating validated Schema.org entity metadata and structured content outlines to assist traditional search engines and answer engines (AEO) in accurately understanding site content.

We enforce strict architectural boundaries on all AI deployments: client proprietary data is never used for external model training, non-deterministic outputs are bounded by runtime validation schemas (Zod), and critical actions always retain human-in-the-loop escalation paths.
