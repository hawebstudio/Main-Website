# Anthropic Claude
## What Is Anthropic Claude?

Claude is a family of AI models built by Anthropic, known for strong reasoning ability, careful handling of instructions, and a strong safety-conscious design approach. Available through Anthropic's API, Claude can be integrated into custom applications for tasks like content generation, document analysis, and conversational assistants.

At HA Web Studio, we integrate Claude for projects where careful, well-reasoned responses matter, particularly for customer-facing tools and content-related tasks.

## Why We Use Anthropic Claude at HA Web Studio

- Strong performance on reasoning-heavy and instruction-following tasks
- Large context window for processing long documents or conversations
- Careful, safety-conscious design suited to customer-facing applications
- Solid coding and technical writing capability for internal tooling
- A strong option alongside OpenAI and Gemini depending on the task

## How Anthropic Claude Helps Your Business

For customer-facing AI features, the quality and reliability of responses directly affects trust in your brand. Claude's careful approach to following instructions and staying within defined boundaries makes it a strong fit for assistants that need to represent your business accurately and avoid overstepping their intended role.

Its large context window is also useful for tasks like analyzing long documents, contracts, reports, transcripts, or maintaining coherent, longer conversations in a support or advisory tool.

## Anthropic Claude Use Cases We Build

- Customer-facing assistants that need careful, on-brand responses
- Document and contract analysis tools
- Content drafting and editing assistants
- Internal knowledge base question-answering tools
- Technical writing and documentation assistance

## Anthropic Claude + Our Stack

We integrate Claude through Anthropic's API, often via the Vercel AI SDK for a consistent interface across providers, and through LangChain or a direct Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration when a project needs Claude to connect with your specific tools and data sources.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Claude better than OpenAI's models?**
Neither is universally better, each has particular strengths. We recommend a model based on your specific use case, not a blanket preference.

**Can Claude access our company's specific data?**
Yes, through retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) or MCP integrations that connect Claude to your documents and systems in a controlled way.

**Is this safe for customer-facing use?**
Yes, with proper guardrails and testing, which we build into every AI feature we deliver, regardless of the underlying model.
