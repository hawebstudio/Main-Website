# Razorpay

## Overview

Razorpay is a payment processing platform built specifically for the Indian market, supporting cards, UPI, net banking, wallets, and EMI options through a single integration. It handles the compliance, security, and settlement complexity of accepting digital payments in India.

## What Is Razorpay?

Razorpay is a payment processing platform built specifically for the Indian market, supporting cards, UPI, net banking, wallets, and EMI options through a single integration. It handles the compliance, security, and settlement complexity of accepting digital payments in India.

At HA Web Studio, Razorpay is our recommended payment integration for businesses selling primarily to Indian customers, given its deep support for the payment methods Indian shoppers actually prefer.

## Why We Use Razorpay at HA Web Studio

- Native support for UPI, one of India's most-used payment methods
- Wide coverage of cards, wallets, net banking, and EMI options
- Compliance with Indian regulatory and RBI requirements
- Competitive local pricing compared to international-first processors
- Strong documentation and developer tools for clean integration

## How Razorpay Helps Your Business

Offering the payment methods your customers actually prefer, UPI in particular, has a direct, measurable effect on checkout completion in the Indian market. A checkout that only supports cards leaves a significant share of potential customers unable to pay the way they want to.

Razorpay's local compliance handling also reduces the regulatory burden on your business, since the platform is built specifically around Indian payment regulations rather than adapted from a global-first product.

## Razorpay Use Cases We Build

- Checkout flows optimized for Indian shoppers (UPI, cards, wallets)
- Subscription billing for Indian customer bases
- Marketplace payouts to Indian vendors and partners
- Invoicing and payment links for service-based businesses
- EMI and buy-now-pay-later checkout options

## Razorpay + Our Stack

We integrate Razorpay through Node.js and Express, with webhook handling to keep order and payment status in sync automatically, and React-based checkout interfaces built with React Hook Form and Zod for validation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Should we use Razorpay or Stripe for our business?**
If most of your customers are in India, Razorpay's local payment method support (especially UPI) typically improves conversion. For international-first businesses, Stripe is usually the better fit.

**Does Razorpay support subscriptions?**
Yes, including recurring billing and automated payment collection.

**Is Razorpay compliant with Indian regulations?**
Yes, it's built specifically around RBI and Indian regulatory requirements.
