## What Is WooCommerce?

WooCommerce is an e-commerce plugin that turns a WordPress website into a fully functional online store, products, cart, checkout, and order management, while keeping all the content and blogging capabilities WordPress is known for.

At HA Web Studio, we build WooCommerce stores for businesses that want e-commerce and content marketing living in the same platform, managed by a team that's already comfortable with WordPress.

## Why We Use WooCommerce at HA Web Studio

- E-commerce and content management in a single platform
- Highly customizable through WordPress's plugin ecosystem
- Full ownership of your store's code and data, no platform lock-in

## How WooCommerce Helps Your Business

For businesses built around content, blogs, guides, brand storytelling, alongside product sales, WooCommerce keeps everything in one system rather than juggling a separate CMS and store platform. Your team manages products, blog posts, and pages from the same familiar WordPress dashboard.

Because WooCommerce is self-hosted, you also have full control over your data and aren't dependent on a third-party platform's pricing changes or policy decisions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is WooCommerce as fast as Shopify?**
Out of the box, WooCommerce sites can be slower due to how WordPress and plugins interact. We specifically optimize performance during build, caching, image optimization, hosting choice, to close that gap.

**Do we own our store's data with WooCommerce?**
Yes, since it's self-hosted on WordPress, you have full control over your data, unlike hosted platforms.

**Is WooCommerce good for a large catalog?**
It handles mid-sized catalogs well; very large catalogs may need additional performance optimization, which we plan for during the build.
