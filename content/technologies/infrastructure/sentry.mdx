# Sentry
## What Is Sentry?

Sentry is an error monitoring and performance tracking tool that automatically detects when something goes wrong on your website or application, a broken checkout flow, a failed API call, a JavaScript error, and alerts your team with detailed information about exactly what happened and where.

At HA Web Studio, we integrate Sentry so issues are caught and reported in real time, often before a customer even reports a problem themselves.

## Why We Use Sentry at HA Web Studio

- Real-time alerts when errors occur on your live website
- Detailed error context, making issues fast to diagnose and fix
- Performance monitoring to catch slowdowns before they affect many users
- Reduces reliance on customers reporting problems themselves
- Historical error tracking to spot recurring or worsening issues

## How Sentry Helps Your Business

Most businesses only find out about a broken feature when a frustrated customer reports it, by which point it may have already affected many visitors and cost real business. Sentry catches these issues the moment they happen, often letting us fix a problem before it meaningfully affects your traffic or reputation.

Detailed error reports also mean faster fixes: instead of trying to reproduce a vague customer complaint, our team gets the exact technical context needed to resolve the issue quickly.

## Sentry Use Cases We Build

- Real-time error alerts for checkout, forms, and critical user flows
- Performance monitoring for pages that may be slowing down over time
- Tracking errors across both frontend (React) and backend (Node.js) code
- Historical trend monitoring to catch recurring issues
- Release tracking to correlate new errors with specific deployments

## Sentry + Our Stack

Sentry monitors both our React/Next.js frontends and Node.js/Express backends, integrated into the GitHub Actions deployment pipeline so new releases are automatically tracked against any errors they introduce.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will this notify us immediately if something breaks?**
Yes, Sentry sends real-time alerts, so issues are typically caught and addressed quickly, often before significant impact.

**Do we need to monitor this ourselves constantly?**
No, we can configure alerting and, depending on your maintenance plan, monitor and respond to issues on your behalf.

**Is this only useful for large, complex applications?**
No, even a simple business website benefits from knowing immediately if a contact form or checkout flow breaks.
