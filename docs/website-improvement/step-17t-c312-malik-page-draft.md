### Section: Engineering Principles
1. **Commercial Clarity Before Code**: Technology exists to solve a specific operational or commercial challenge. We design architectures around your actual customer funnel, not around transient framework hype.
2. **Speed & Accessibility by Default**: Sub-second loading, strict Core Web Vitals compliance, and WCAG-aligned semantics are not add-on packages—they are built into every component and layout from day one.
3. **Maintainable Handoffs**: We build systems your internal team or future developers can easily understand and evolve. No opaque black boxes, no artificial vendor lock-in.

### Section: What We Take On (and What We Don't)
- **High-Fit Projects**:
  - Custom Next.js web applications, internal tools, and SaaS MVPs.
  - Performance-critical B2B websites and commercial redesigns.
  - Modern e-commerce architectures (custom Shopify themes and headless integrations).
  - Technical SEO infrastructure and Core Web Vitals remediation.
- **Where We Advise Alternate Paths**:
  - Low-budget brochure sites that are better served by standard no-code builders (e.g. Webflow, Squarespace).
  - High-volume catalog stores requiring deep off-the-shelf app ecosystems where native Shopify Liquid is faster and more cost-effective than custom headless.
  - Speculative architectures with no clear business model or audience validation.

### Section: Working Together
Direct communication, transparent milestones, and zero fluff. You work directly with the engineer making architectural decisions—ensuring fast feedback loops and complete accountability from discovery to deployment.
